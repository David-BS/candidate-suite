# Structure of the 2 interviews

This reference defines the question types to generate for each interview
and the answer best practices.

## Interview 1 — Screening (Motivation)

### Context
First interview, often with an HR recruiter or a manager. Typical length: 30-45 min.
**Recruiter's goal**: validate the candidate's sincere interest, motivation,
cultural fit, and screen them before the deeper technical interviews.

### Question types to generate (8-10 max)

#### Block A — Motivation and fit
1. **Why this role?** ("Why did you apply to this posting?")
2. **Why this company?** ("What attracts you to [company]?")
3. **What do you know about us?** (a test of preparation and seriousness)
4. **Why want to change roles?** (if applicable)

#### Block B — Background and self-positioning
5. **Tell me about yourself** (1-2 minute presentation max)
6. **What are your strengths?** (top 3)
7. **What are your weaknesses / development areas?**
8. **Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?**

#### Block C — Practical
9. **What's your availability?**
10. **What are your salary expectations?** (if raised, otherwise deflect politely)

### Answer best practices for the screening

- **Authentic**: don't recite, speak with conviction
- **Company-specific**: mention concrete elements (mission, known projects, values)
- **Short**: 30-90 seconds per answer out loud, so 3-6 written sentences as a memory aid
- **Connect to the CV**: reference a relevant experience
- **Strengths/weaknesses**: strengths 3 + 1 concrete example; for weaknesses pick a real one but with an improvement axis under way

### Screening Q&A example (French)

> **Q : Pourquoi avez-vous postulé à ce poste de Senior Engineering Manager chez Acme Corp ?**
>
> R : Trois raisons. D'abord, le poste correspond à mon expérience actuelle de pilotage d'équipes engineering en fintech. Ensuite, Acme Corp est reconnu pour son approche [cloud-native / sustainable tech / X] qui résonne avec mes convictions techniques. Enfin, j'ai identifié dans l'offre des défis concrets — comme [élément spécifique de l'offre] — sur lesquels mon expérience de [réalisation passée chiffrée] m'a préparé à contribuer rapidement.

### Screening Q&A example (English)

> **Q: What attracts you to this Senior Engineering Manager role at Acme Corp?**
>
> A: Three reasons. First, the role matches my current experience leading engineering teams in fintech. Second, Acme Corp is known for its [cloud-native / sustainable tech / X] approach, which aligns with my technical convictions. Third, I've identified specific challenges in the job description — such as [specific element from the offer] — where my past experience of [past achievement with number] has prepared me to contribute quickly.

## Interview 2 — Skills validation

### Context
A more technical interview, often with a direct manager, a senior peer, or a panel.
Typical length: 45-90 min.
**Recruiter's goal**: validate that the candidate **truly masters** the expected
technical/domain skills and can tell their experiences with depth.

### Question types to generate (8-10 max)

#### Block A — Technical / domain skills
**Identify 4-5 key skills mentioned in the posting** and generate one question per skill:
1. Question on skill #1 ("How did you tackle [X] in your current role?")
2. Question on skill #2
3. Question on skill #3
4. Question on skill #4

#### Block B — Case studies / achievements
5. **Tell me about a project you're most proud of** (in STAR format)
6. **Describe a difficult situation and how you solved it**
7. **What's your biggest quantified achievement?**

#### Block C — Leadership / methodology (if managerial or senior role)
8. **How do you structure your team / your priorities?**
9. **How do you handle a conflict within the team?**
10. **What's your approach to [key methodology from the posting — agile, scaled agile, etc.]?**

### Answer best practices for skills validation

- **STAR format**: Situation / Task / Action / Result
  - **Situation**: quick context (1-2 sentences)
  - **Task**: your role, the challenge
  - **Action**: what YOU did (in the "I," not the "we")
  - **Result**: quantified impact if possible
- **Specific**: avoid generalities, give figures and concrete examples
- **Honest**: don't invent skills absent from the CV
- **Longer than for the screening**: 1-3 minutes per answer, so 5-10 sentences as a memory aid
- **Anticipate follow-up questions**: prepare 1-2 extra details per topic

### Skills-validation Q&A example (French)

> **Q : Pouvez-vous me parler d'un projet de modernisation cloud-native que vous avez piloté, avec les défis rencontrés et les résultats obtenus ?**
>
> R :
> - **Situation** : Chez Globex, nous avions une plateforme de paiement legacy en monolithe qui limitait notre capacité à scaler et à livrer rapidement.
> - **Tâche** : J'ai été nommé responsable de la modernisation cloud-native sur 18 mois, avec une équipe de 12 ingénieurs.
> - **Action** : J'ai d'abord cartographié les dépendances métier critiques, puis structuré la migration en 4 phases (extraction des bounded contexts, refactoring vers Kubernetes, migration des données, décommissionnement). J'ai aussi mis en place un système de feature flags pour limiter les risques.
> - **Résultat** : Migration complète sans incident majeur, réduction des coûts d'infrastructure de 35%, cycles de release passés de 2 semaines à 2 jours, et amélioration de la disponibilité à 99,99%.

### Skills-validation Q&A example (English)

> **Q: Can you tell me about a cloud-native modernization project you led, including the challenges and outcomes?**
>
> A:
> - **Situation**: At Globex, our legacy monolithic payment platform was limiting our ability to scale and ship quickly.
> - **Task**: I was appointed lead for the cloud-native modernization over 18 months, managing a team of 12 engineers.
> - **Action**: I first mapped the critical business dependencies, then structured the migration in 4 phases (bounded context extraction, Kubernetes refactoring, data migration, decommissioning). I also implemented a feature flag system to mitigate risks.
> - **Result**: Complete migration without major incident, 35% infrastructure cost reduction, release cycles cut from 2 weeks to 2 days, and improved availability to 99.99%.

## Personalization rules

### Mandatory source of answers
- **CV**: use the real experiences, roles, companies, years
- **Posting**: align the highlighted skills with those expected
- **NEVER** invent an experience or an achievement absent from the CV

### Adapting to the role's level
- **Junior**: less leadership, more fundamentals, eagerness to learn
- **Mid-level**: balance of technical + autonomy + team impact
- **Senior / Lead**: leadership, strategy, mentoring, long-term vision
- **Director / Head of**: business vision, leadership of teams-of-teams, ROI

### Adapting to the language
- **French**: vouvoiement, non-breaking spaces before `: ; ? !`
- **English**: plain "you," direct punctuation

### Consistency with the cover letter
If a cover letter has already been generated for this application, the answers must be
**aligned** with what it says (same quantified achievements, same positioning).
