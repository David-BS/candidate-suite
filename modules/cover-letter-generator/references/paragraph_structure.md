# Structure of the cover letter's 5 paragraphs

⚠️ This structure is **mandatory**. Do not deviate from it. *(It is the frozen-structure
counterweight of LNG-1: the section set is fixed; only the wording is produced by the model.)*

## Guiding principle

The cover letter must progress from:
- **§1**: Who I am and why this role interests me
- **§2-3**: My background that legitimizes me
- **§4**: **WHAT I CONCRETELY BRING to YOU** ← the heart of the value
- **§5**: Why YOU, and call to action

**§4 is the pivot**: it shifts from "here is my CV" to "here is what I will bring you."

---

## §1 — Hook

### Goal
Grab attention in 3-4 sentences: state the target role, signal the relevant years of
experience, and open the fit.

### Expected content
- Explicit mention of the **exact role title** (as written in the posting)
- Mention of the **company**
- **Years of experience** in the relevant field
- A **fit-opening sentence** (one key trait that matches the posting)

### Example (EN)
> "With 12 years of experience leading engineering teams in fintech, I am excited to apply for the Senior Engineering Manager position at Acme Corp. My background in scaling distributed systems and mentoring high-performing teams aligns directly with the requirements outlined in your job description."

### Example (FR)
> "Fort de 12 ans d'expérience dans le pilotage d'équipes d'ingénierie en fintech, je suis enthousiaste à l'idée de postuler au poste de Senior Engineering Manager chez Acme Corp. Mon parcours dans la mise à l'échelle de systèmes distribués et l'accompagnement d'équipes performantes correspond directement aux attentes de votre annonce."

### Avoid
- ❌ "My name is X and I'm applying for..." (bland)
- ❌ A long unrelated preamble
- ❌ Lying about the number of years of experience

---

## §2 — Current role

### Goal
Show what you do **now** and draw a **direct parallel** with the target role.

### Expected content
- **Current role** (title + company)
- **2-3 key responsibilities** aligned with the posting's needs
- **One or two notable achievements** in this role (with figures if possible)
- A **transition sentence** showing the link to the target role

### Example (EN)
> "In my current role as Engineering Director at Globex Industries, I oversee a team of 25 engineers across 4 squads, owning the architecture and delivery of our core payment platform processing €5B+ annually. I have led the modernization of legacy systems toward cloud-native architectures, reducing infrastructure costs by 35% while improving system reliability to 99.99% uptime. These responsibilities directly mirror the leadership scope and technical challenges described in your role."

### Example (FR)
> "Dans mon rôle actuel de Directeur Ingénierie chez Globex Industries, je supervise une équipe de 25 ingénieurs répartis sur 4 squads, et je suis responsable de l'architecture et de la livraison de notre plateforme de paiement traitant plus de 5 milliards d'euros annuels. J'ai piloté la modernisation des systèmes legacy vers des architectures cloud-native, réduisant les coûts d'infrastructure de 35 % tout en améliorant la fiabilité du système à 99,99 % de disponibilité. Ces responsabilités font directement écho au périmètre de leadership et aux défis techniques décrits dans votre annonce."

### Avoid
- ❌ Listing ALL your responsibilities (too heavy)
- ❌ No figures / impact (bland)
- ❌ No link to the target role

---

## §3 — Relevant prior experience

### Goal
Reinforce legitimacy with **past experiences** that consolidate the application and add an
extra dimension.

### Expected content
- **1-2 relevant prior experiences** (significant companies or projects)
- Highlighting of complementary **technical or managerial skills**
- Notable **achievements** (with figures if possible)
- Bonus: variety of environments (startup → large group, etc.) if relevant

### Example (EN)
> "Prior to Globex, I spent 6 years at Initech building cloud-native applications, where I developed deep expertise in Kubernetes, microservices, and CI/CD pipelines. I also led the launch of a B2B SaaS product that grew from 0 to 50,000 active users in 18 months. Earlier in my career at TechStart, I had the opportunity to build engineering practices from scratch in a fast-paced startup environment — an experience that shaped my pragmatic approach to scaling."

### Example (FR)
> "Avant Globex, j'ai passé 6 ans chez Initech à construire des applications cloud-native, où j'ai développé une expertise approfondie en Kubernetes, microservices et pipelines CI/CD. J'y ai également piloté le lancement d'un produit SaaS B2B passé de 0 à 50 000 utilisateurs actifs en 18 mois. Plus tôt dans ma carrière chez TechStart, j'ai eu l'opportunité de bâtir des pratiques d'ingénierie depuis zéro dans un environnement startup en croissance rapide — une expérience qui a forgé mon approche pragmatique du scaling."

### Avoid
- ❌ Redoing a full chronological CV
- ❌ Detailing each role with no link to the posting
- ❌ Mentioning experiences with no added value for the application

## §4 — Value proposition ⭐ (key paragraph)

### Goal
**The letter's pivot.** Clearly articulate **what the candidate will bring to the company**.
Oriented toward **benefit for the employer**, not a self-portrait of the candidate.

### Crucial difference
- ❌ "I did X, Y, Z" (past, self-centered)
- ✅ "I will bring X, Y, Z to your company, as my past achievements illustrate" (future, employer-centered)

### Expected content
- **2-3 concrete benefits** the candidate will bring (not a list of skills)
- Each benefit **tied to a challenge/stake** identified in the posting or typical of the company's context
- **Quantified proof from the past** as a guarantee of the ability to deliver
- **Employer-oriented phrasing stance** (future contribution framing — *what I will bring / how
  your team will benefit*). The specific phrasings are realized **natively in the target
  language** — see `letter_conventions.md` (§4 stance); do not import ready-made phrasings from
  another language. *(This replaces the former FR/EN turn-of-phrase list, per LNG-1 L3.)*

### Typical structure
1. An opening sentence announcing the value you bring
2. Benefit 1 (with quantified proof)
3. Benefit 2 (with quantified proof)
4. (Optional) Benefit 3 or a synthesis of the approach

### Example (EN)
> "Beyond my technical and managerial background, what I bring to Acme Corp is a proven ability to translate complex business challenges into actionable engineering roadmaps. Concretely, I can help your team accelerate platform modernization while protecting reliability — an approach that delivered 40% faster release cycles and zero major incidents over the past two years at Globex. I will also strengthen your engineering culture through structured mentoring and clear technical decision-making frameworks, which have helped me retain over 90% of senior engineers in my teams. Ultimately, my contribution will be tangible: faster delivery, stronger teams, and resilient systems aligned with your growth ambitions."

### Example (FR)
> "Au-delà de mon parcours technique et managérial, ce que j'apporterai à Acme Corp, c'est une capacité éprouvée à traduire des défis métier complexes en feuilles de route ingénierie actionnables. Concrètement, je pourrai aider votre équipe à accélérer la modernisation de la plateforme tout en préservant la fiabilité — une approche qui a permis chez Globex 40 % de cycles de livraison plus rapides et zéro incident majeur sur les deux dernières années. Je renforcerai également votre culture d'ingénierie via un mentorat structuré et des cadres clairs de décision technique, qui m'ont permis de retenir plus de 90 % de mes ingénieurs seniors. In fine, ma contribution sera tangible : livraisons plus rapides, équipes renforcées, systèmes résilients alignés avec vos ambitions de croissance."

### Avoid
- ❌ Restating past achievements without projecting them onto the target company (= duplicate of §2-3)
- ❌ Listing skills ("I master A, B, C") with no link to a benefit
- ❌ Vague and generic ("I'm motivated and I like a challenge")
- ❌ Unsupported promises ("I'll transform your company" with no proof)

### Test question to validate §4
*"If the recruiter reads only this paragraph, do they concretely understand what this candidate
will bring them and why they should meet them?"*

If the answer is no → rewrite.

## §5 — Conclusion

### Goal
Close with a **specific motivation for THIS company** (not generic) and invite a conversation.

### Expected content
- **Specific mention of the company**: values, mission, recent news, identifiable project. Not generic.
- **Why this company specifically** (and not another in the same sector)
- **Availability** (immediate, or a date)
- **Call to action**: interview, conversation
- A polite **thank-you**

### Example (EN)
> "I am particularly drawn to Acme Corp's commitment to sustainability in tech infrastructure — a topic that has shaped my recent technical decisions and that I'm eager to push further with your team. I am available for an interview at your convenience and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience could support Acme Corp's next chapter. Thank you for considering my application."

### Example (FR)
> "Je suis particulièrement attiré par l'engagement d'Acme Corp en faveur de la durabilité des infrastructures tech — un sujet qui guide mes choix techniques récents et que j'aimerais pousser plus loin avec votre équipe. Je suis disponible pour un entretien à votre convenance et serais ravi de discuter de la manière dont mon expérience peut soutenir le prochain chapitre d'Acme Corp. Je vous remercie de l'attention portée à ma candidature."

### Avoid
- ❌ "I am interested in your company" (empty, generic)
- ❌ "I await your reply" (passive)
- ❌ A too-long conclusion (3-4 sentences max)
- ❌ No specific reference to the company

## Overall coherence

### Target length
- **§1**: 3-4 sentences (~70-90 words)
- **§2**: 4-5 sentences (~100-130 words)
- **§3**: 3-5 sentences (~80-120 words)
- **§4**: 4-6 sentences (~120-160 words) — **the most important**
- **§5**: 3-4 sentences (~70-100 words)

**Total**: ~440-600 words → fits on one A4 page with the spacing and the address block.

### Tone
- Professional but not stiff
- Assertive without arrogance
- Specific (figures, concrete examples) without overloading
- Consistent with the seniority level of the target role

### Coherence between paragraphs
- §2 and §3 must not contradict each other (e.g. years of experience)
- §4 must build on the elements of §2-3 (the quantified proof must come from the experiences mentioned)
- §5 must echo §1 (the target role), closing the loop

### Personalization
- Each cover letter must be **adapted to the posting**: do not generate a recycled generic letter
- Explicitly mention **elements from the posting** (technologies, mission, challenges)
- Include the company name in **§1 and §5** at minimum
